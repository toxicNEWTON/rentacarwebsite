SHAHG RENT A CAR — WEBSITE PACKAGE
=================================

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP file completely.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
3. Keep the assets folder beside index.html.

FOLDER STRUCTURE
index.html               Website page
assets/css/style.css     Page styles
assets/js/main.js        Car data, prices, calculator, contact form logic
assets/images/           10 bundled illustrative car images + hero image

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING
- Images are AI-generated illustrative pictures, NOT verified photos of the actual listed rental cars. Replace with photos of your actual fleet before advertising specific vehicles.
- All rates are DEMO prices. Update them in assets/js/main.js in CONFIG.cars.
- WhatsApp number and business email are blank. Set CONFIG.whatsappNumber and CONFIG.businessEmail in assets/js/main.js.
- Contact form uses FormSubmit and needs internet, a real business email and FormSubmit first-time verification. The website otherwise works offline including car browsing and rent calculator.
- There is no backend or payment processing; rental requests do not create confirmed reservations.
- Deploy the complete folder contents to any static web host; upload index.html and assets/ together.
